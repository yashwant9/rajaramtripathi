Rooted in Bastar.

The journey began in the forests of Bastar, where farming was deeply connected to nature, tradition, and community life.

Observing these systems closely revealed a powerful truth — that agriculture, when done right, can sustain not just land, but livelihoods and dignity.

This understanding became the foundation of a lifelong mission: to create farming models that are economically viable, environmentally sustainable, and scalable for the future.

Rajaram Tripathi's Legacy

Over the years, this belief has taken shape through interconnected systems — linking farming, conservation, and market access.

Herbal Value Chain

Through Maa Danteshwari Herbal Products, a complete ecosystem has been developed — connecting cultivation to processing, manufacturing, and global markets.

Farming Innovation

High Value Multi Layer Farming (HVML) rethinks how land is used — enabling multiple crops, better yield, and long-term soil health within a single integrated system.

Biodiversity Conservation

Over 250 rare and medicinal plant species are actively preserved and cultivated, creating a living repository of traditional botanical knowledge.

Farmer Network

A growing network of over 80,000 farmers has been trained and supported to adopt sustainable and profitable agricultural practices.

About Dr. Rajaram Tripathi

Herbal Farmer • Scientist • System Builder

Dr. Rajaram Tripathi is widely regarded as one of India's leading voices in organic and herbal farming — often referred to as the Green Warrior of India.

His work bridges traditional knowledge with scientific farming practices, creating systems that are both environmentally sustainable and economically viable.

ENVIRONMENT & BIODIVERSITY

A strong advocate of nature-led farming, Dr. Tripathi believes that environmental protection is only possible when natural ecosystems are allowed to grow and function organically.

  • Conserved 250+ rare medicinal plant species, many listed in the United Nations Red Data Book
  • Planted 3.4 lakh+ trees, contributing significantly to ecological restoration
  • Developed the Ethnico Medico Park, a living herbal forest preserving endangered species

FARMING INNOVATION & APPROACH

Dr. Tripathi has redefined conventional agricultural practices by introducing scientific and ecological methods into farming.

His approach challenges traditional assumptions — for instance, even termites, typically seen as harmful, are integrated into the ecosystem for their role in improving soil nutrition.

These innovations demonstrate how natural processes can be leveraged rather than controlled.

MARKET & VALUE CHAIN

Understanding that farming must connect to real economic outcomes, he built a complete herbal value chain through Maa Danteshwari Herbal Products.

This ecosystem integrates:

Cultivation Processing Manufacturing Export

A Working Model of Sustainable Farming

Developed over decades, this model integrates biodiversity, scientific methods, and market linkage — demonstrating how farming can be both sustainable and profitable.

Under the supervision of Tripathi, 34 acres of land in Kondagaon is being cultivated extensively by certified organic method, along with this black pepper and bitterness stevia is cultivated on large scale. In order to extend this model, sustainable organic farming is being done on 1100 acres of land in Bastar region with several farmer groups. Biodiversity is clearly reflected in Maa Danteshwari Herbal Farm and is a special farm of its kind in the country. In the villages of Bastar region, Salna, Palna, Marangpuri, Randhana, Makdi, Chikalputi, Sambalpur, Chapka, Poleng, and Karanji villages, organic farming and vegetable cultivation is being done on 700 acres with help of farmers groups. Dr. Tripathi has been involved in farming since 1996 and in these 33 years he has presented an exemplary model of farming.

Maa Danteshwari Herbal Farm is the only farm where biodiversity creates an attractive view and presents an example of profitable farming. In the farm, 3.4 lakh trees and more than 240 kinds of Medicinal herbs, spices, Aromatic Farming and other high value crops, Germ plaza are cultivated through the crop cycle. Crop pests in the farm are managed with biological technology, due to which crop-based pests are highly inhabited. He has promote the Organic Cultivation of Indian Medicinal and Aromatic Crops and Establishment of Ethno Medico Forest.

For the last 20 years, farmers of various regions of the country are getting free training on organic farming, cultivation of medicinal plants, making of organic manure, organic pesticides, and fungicides. So far, more than 70 thousand farmers have been trained.

Training on low cost tissue culture technic is given in the Natural Effective Micro Manufacturing Unit, Kondagaon Industrial Area.

Presented a model of high benefit from Australian teak, a partner in nitrogen fixation.

Post harvesting, packaging and marketing training is being given in Maa Danteshwari Herbal Processing Industry located in Kondagaon Industrial Area.
